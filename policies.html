Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information is collected through this website, why it's collected, and how it's used. It's written to align with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Information collected

  • Name, phone number and/or email address, submitted through the booking form or contact links
  • Project details you choose to share (business name, description, timeline, budget range)
  • Basic technical data collected by your browser when loading external fonts (see "Third parties" below)

How it's used

  • To respond to project enquiries and prepare quotes
  • To deliver the design services you've requested
  • To keep a record of past client communication for invoicing and support

Information is never sold, rented, or used for purposes beyond responding to your enquiry and delivering agreed work.

How the booking form works

This site has no server or database. When you submit the booking form, your details are formatted into a message and handed to WhatsApp or your email app to send — nothing is transmitted or stored by this website itself. Once sent, that message is held by WhatsApp (Meta) or your email provider under their respective privacy terms.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts — typefaces are loaded from Google's servers, which may log your IP address as part of the request
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — used to receive booking messages you choose to send
  • Email provider — used to receive booking messages you choose to send via email

Your rights

Under POPIA, you can ask to see what information is held about you, request corrections, or ask for it to be deleted, unless it's needed to complete an active or recently completed project (e.g. for invoicing records). To make a request, contact masileasandile01@gmail.com.

Data retention

Project-related messages and files are kept for as long as reasonably needed to deliver the work and maintain records for tax and dispute purposes, and are deleted or anonymised after that unless you request otherwise.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to masileasandile01@gmail.com or via WhatsApp.

Terms of Service

These terms apply to any design, web, or marketing project booked through this website or agreed directly with Sandile Masilela ("the designer").

Scope of work

Each project is defined by a written scope (via WhatsApp, email, or a formal proposal) agreed before work begins, including deliverables, timeline, and price. Work outside that scope is treated as a separate, additional request.

Payment

  • A non-refundable deposit (typically 50%) is required before design work begins, unless otherwise agreed in writing
  • The remaining balance is due on delivery of final files, before high-resolution or source files are handed over
  • Ongoing/monthly packages are billed at the start of each month and may be paused or cancelled with 14 days' notice

Revisions

Each package includes a set number of revision rounds, stated at proposal stage. Additional revisions beyond that are billed at an hourly rate, agreed in advance.

Intellectual property

  • Full ownership and usage rights of final deliverables transfer to the client once the project is paid in full
  • Until final payment is received, all designs remain the property of the designer and may not be used commercially
  • The designer retains the right to display completed work in a portfolio, unless the client requests confidentiality in writing

Timelines

Timelines are estimates based on the agreed scope and timely feedback from the client. Delays in client feedback, content, or approvals will extend the delivery date accordingly.

Liability

The designer will deliver work with reasonable skill and care, but is not liable for indirect losses (e.g. lost business, lost revenue) arising from the use of delivered designs. Clients are responsible for reviewing and approving final files — including spelling, contact details, and legal compliance — before publishing or printing.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of South Africa.

Booking & Cancellation Policy

This covers how project bookings, consultations, and cancellations work.

Making a booking

Submitting the booking form sends a project request via WhatsApp or email — it is a request, not a confirmed booking. A project is confirmed once scope, timeline and price are agreed and, where required, the deposit is received.

Response times

Enquiries are typically responded to within 24–48 hours on business days. A free 15-minute intro consultation can be requested for new projects; consultations beyond that are billed at R250/hour.

Cancelling a project

  • Cancelling before work begins: deposit is non-refundable, as it secures your project slot
  • Cancelling after work begins: the deposit plus a pro-rated fee for work completed to that point is due
  • Cancelling a monthly package: takes effect at the end of the current billing month, with 14 days' notice

Rescheduling

Timelines can be moved by mutual agreement, with as much notice as possible. Repeated rescheduling on the client's side beyond two occasions may require a new proposal and timeline.

Refunds

Deposits are non-refundable once a project slot is secured. If a project is cancelled by the designer before any work has started, the full deposit is refunded.

Communication

Project updates are primarily handled over WhatsApp or email, based on your preference selected at booking. Please keep contact details up to date so updates aren't missed.
