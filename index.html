About

Strategy first, then the visuals.

I'm a brand identity designer based in Johannesburg with a focus on helping South African businesses stand out in competitive markets.

My approach goes beyond making things look good — every decision is backed by business strategy, whether that's building trust through professional branding, improving usability to lift conversions, or producing marketing material that actually gets engaged with.

I've worked across construction, automotive, education technology and personal-care brands, which gives me a working sense of what different markets actually need to succeed.
